# Limitations

- All transactions are synthetic.
- The ruleset is intentionally compact and does not represent a complete enterprise data-quality framework.
- SQLite persists evidence locally; distributed lineage, cataloguing and orchestration are outside scope.
- Quarantine is row-based and does not model field-level remediation workflows or stewardship queues.
- The release gate is deterministic and does not implement human approvals, SLAs or exception waivers.
- Alerting, ticketing, observability and production incident integrations are not implemented.
- No realised reduction in incidents, reporting errors or business losses is claimed.
